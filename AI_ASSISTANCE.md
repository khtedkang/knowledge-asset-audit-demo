# AI assistance disclosure

This portfolio demonstration was developed with AI assistance under Ted
Kang's direction. The human role included defining the problem, setting the
confidentiality boundary, selecting the audit controls, reviewing the code and
outputs, and accepting the final result. AI assistance was used to accelerate
implementation, documentation, test creation, and review.

The demonstration is intentionally clean-room and synthetic. It does not
contain employer files, source code, internal prompts, customer or
partner data, unpublished materials, or other company information.

The software produces decision-support metadata. Its classifications and
lifecycle hints are deterministic heuristics, not legal, records-management,
security, or compliance determinations. A human should review results before
taking action on real files.
